## 2. Integration, principal revisions and final verification

Dũng's financial dataset, Hiền's earlier rules and Ngọc's scenarios provided the initial foundations. **Phương and Khuê carried the two main areas of reconstruction and final production:**

- **Phương - investigation and logic:** rebuilt the five-chapter investigation through Draft 1, Draft 2 and the final functional prototype; redesigned how evidence supports findings, passes between chapters and contributes to assessment. During production, she checked and updated figures and content for Khuê to implement. **H03–H05; C01a–C01c; F1.**
- **Khuê - playable game and technical implementation:** turned the prototype into the complete game, designing the visual environment and interactions, implementing and revising the play flow, integrating graphics, sound and video, and packaging the published build. She tested the implementation and corrected errors as updates were incorporated. **B01–B02; C01d, C01f–C01g; D03.**

Their integration work connected the investigation to actual player actions: Phương established the evidence and assessment logic; Khuê implemented how players read records, submit findings, move between chapters and retain results. The final game preserves Phương's investigation framework while using Khuê's redesigned interface and operating flow. The source comparisons document both stages.

For final verification, **Phương checked figures, content and consistency; Khuê checked implementation and player interactions and fixed the code.** Dũng, Hiền and Ngọc contributed the earlier foundations but did not participate in this final game-development and update loop. Week 6 separately records 40 automated logic cases and 18 data checks; it does not identify individual executors, and UI01–UI12 were not run in that review. Its assignment table is not evidence of participation in final production. **W6.**
