## 6. Source Quality Principle

External frameworks provide concepts/formulas.

Case-specific values, approval thresholds, Proxy Route conditions, evidence patterns, system behavior and storyline timing are explicitly simulated.

The project must not present fictional Aster policy as PCAOB, COSO, ACFE or Beneish requirement.

Raw evidence should remain factual. Interpretation and conclusion belong to gameplay reasoning/feedback, not to the source record itself.
